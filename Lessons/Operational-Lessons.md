# Purpose

The Operational Lessons Register (OLR) captures actionable lessons derived from validated Knowledge Objects within the EMO-BoK.

Unlike the EMO-BoK History, which preserves historical analytical assessments, and the Knowledge Object Library, which records enduring professional knowledge, the OLR identifies practical guidance for planners, operators, capability developers and decision-makers.

The OLR represents the operational application of EMO knowledge.

---

# Scope

Included

- Operational lessons
- Capability lessons
- Planning lessons
- Force development lessons
- Decision support lessons

Excluded

- Article summaries
- Historical assessments
- Knowledge statements
- Technical implementation guidance
- Governance

## OLR-0001

### Lesson

Plan for continuous competition within the electromagnetic environment rather than assuming spectrum contestation begins only during conflict.

### Why It Matters

Persistent EMS awareness improves preparedness, resilience and operational agility.

### Lesson
