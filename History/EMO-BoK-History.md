# Purpose

The EMO-BoK History is the authoritative historical record of professional analytical assessments conducted during the development of the Electromagnetic Operations Body of Knowledge (EMO-BoK).

Its purpose is to preserve the analytical conclusions and professional judgement derived from historical evidence assessments. It is **not** intended to duplicate source articles or become a literature repository.

This document forms the provenance layer of the EMO-BoK.

## BKH-0001

### Source

BAE Systems – The Uncrewed Threat Network (UTN)

### Executive Assessment

The assessment concluded that the report should be interpreted primarily as evidence of evolving NATO and defence-industry thinking rather than as a technical publication. Its principal value lies in demonstrating the transition from platform-centric Counter-UAS approaches towards integrated decision-centric operational architectures.

### Knowledge Gained

- Counter-UAS is fundamentally an integration challenge.
- Decision-centric architectures outperform isolated capability optimisation.
- EM-enabled operational integration is becoming the dominant design philosophy.

### Operational Significance

Future capability development should prioritise architectural coherence, decision support and systems integration over isolated equipment performance.

### Operational Lessons

- Assess Counter-UAS as a system-of-systems capability.
- Prioritise operational architecture.
- Evaluate industry reports for strategic direction as well as technical content.

### Contribution to EMO-BoK

Supports architecture-centric EMO thinking and reinforces integrated operational capability.

### Status

Recovered

## BKH-0002

### Source

Silvus Technologies – EMS White Paper

### Executive Assessment

The assessment concluded that, despite its commercial origin, the paper provided valuable operational insight by framing EMS contestation as an operational manoeuvre problem informed by lessons from Ukraine.

### Knowledge Gained

- EMS manoeuvre is an operational activity.
- Communications resilience underpins mission success.
- Commercial publications can provide enduring conceptual insight.

### Operational Lessons

- Integrate EMS manoeuvre into operational planning.
- Protect communications as combat capability.
- Separate marketing claims from operational evidence.

### Contribution to EMO-BoK

Strengthens the evidence supporting EMS manoeuvre and communications resilience.

### Status

Recovered
