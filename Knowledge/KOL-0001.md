# Purpose

The Knowledge Object Library (KOL) is the authoritative repository of enduring Electromagnetic Operations (EMO) knowledge derived from professional assessments conducted within the EMO-BoK.

Unlike the EMO-BoK History, which preserves analytical provenance, the KOL captures validated knowledge that can be reused across doctrine, capability development, Position Papers, research, the EMO book, EMO-CAS and Microsoft Copilot.

The KOL is the primary intellectual asset of the EMO-BoK.

---

# Scope

Included

- Enduring knowledge
- Validated operational understanding
- Cross-domain EMO principles
- Evidence-supported observations
- Knowledge derived from multiple assessments

Excluded

- Article summaries
- Historical commentary
- Source documents
- Project discussions
- Unverified opinion

# KO-0001

## Title

Continuous Electromagnetic Competition

## Knowledge Statement

The electromagnetic environment should be regarded as continuously contested across competition, crisis and conflict rather than only during declared hostilities.

## Operational Significance

Persistent electromagnetic awareness and adaptation should become routine operational activities.
