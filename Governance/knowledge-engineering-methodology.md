# Knowledge Engineering Methodology
Version: 1.0.0
Status: Authoritative

## Purpose
Describe how evidence is transformed into enduring knowledge within the EMO-BoK.

## Core Philosophy
1. We migrate knowledge, not conversations.
2. Evidence remains evidence.
3. Professional assessment creates knowledge.
4. Knowledge must remain traceable.
5. Confidence grows through converging evidence.

## Knowledge Lifecycle
External Evidence
↓
Professional Assessment
↓
Historical Assessment
↓
Knowledge Object
↓
Operational Lesson
↓
Position Paper
↓
Book

## Repository Objects
### Evidence
External source retained as provenance.

### Historical Assessment
Professional judgement extracted from evidence.

### Knowledge Object
Stable enduring principle. Changes only with stronger evidence.

### Operational Lesson
Actionable guidance derived from Knowledge Objects.

### Position Paper
Synthesis of multiple Knowledge Objects.

### Book
Highest-level synthesis of validated knowledge.

## Confidence Model
★★★★★ Strongly supported
★★★★☆ Multiple independent sources
★★★☆☆ Moderate evidence
★★☆☆☆ Emerging
★☆☆☆☆ Hypothesis

## Governance Rules
- Preserve provenance.
- Reinforce rather than duplicate.
- Never overwrite history.
- Snapshots are immutable once released.
- GitHub is the authoritative repository.
